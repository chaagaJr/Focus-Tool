Calendars, timetable and college

Add calendar links and Focus keeps them in sync every few hours. Classes and shifts give you a heads-up and hold other reminders while they're on. Assignment deadlines become tasks with check-ins planned before them.

    Where do I find the link?
    • Moodle: Calendar → Import or export calendars → Export calendar → choose All events and Recent and next 60 days → Get calendar URL.
    • Brightspace: Calendar → the settings icon → Enable calendar feeds → Subscribe.
    • Canvas: Calendar → Calendar Feed (bottom right).
    • Blackboard: Calendar → Share calendar (or Calendar settings → Get external link).
    • College timetable: most timetable pages have Subscribe, Export or "Add to calendar" with an iCal link. Ask IT services if you can't see one.
    • Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Secret address in iCal format.
    • Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → ICS link.
    • Work rota apps (Deputy, When I Work, Planday and others): look for Calendar sync, Subscribe or iCal in the app's settings.

    Show Focus in your calendar

    Your tasks with times, deadlines, your own events and birthdays, in Apple Calendar or Outlook. Read-only, updates every hour or so.

    Open in Calendar

    iPhone: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the link. Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

    How reminders behave

    Leave a time empty to turn that one off.
